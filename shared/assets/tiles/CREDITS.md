# Tile artwork credits

The 42 SVG tile faces in this folder (34 standard suit/honor tiles used by
Mahjong Table, plus the 8 flower/season bonus tiles additionally used by
Mahjong Solitaire) are sourced from the "Full set" on Wikipedia's
[Mahjong tiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahjong_tiles#Full_set) article,
via Wikimedia Commons.

**License:** [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
**Authors:** Cangjie6 (remake), with original tile designs by Jerry Crimson
Mann and Dewclouds.

Per CC BY-SA, any redistribution of these specific SVG files (including
modified versions) must credit the authors above and remain under a
compatible share-alike license. This does not extend to the rest of this
repository's code, which stays under the project's own MIT license.
