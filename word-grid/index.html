Word Grid

Lay tiles in one row or column to make words that join what's on the board; the first word covers the centre star. Every word you make must be in the word list. Coloured squares double or triple a letter (DL, TL) or the whole word (DW, TW) the first time a tile covers them. Use all seven tiles in one turn for 50 extra. The game ends when someone uses their last tile with the bag empty, or both players pass twice. Words: the public-domain ENABLE list.
