Ludo

Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard. Move clockwise round the board and up your own coloured column, and land exactly on home to finish. Landing on an opponent sends it back to its yard, except on a start square or a star. A 6, a capture or a token getting home earns another roll; a third 6 in a row loses the turn. First to get all four tokens home wins.
