          Rules this quiz uses

          Every hand is a real win, scored by the same engine as the Riichi table. Yaku and dora give han; the hand's groups, wait and how it was won give fu. Base points are fu × 2^(han+2), capped at mangan. When a hand can be read more than one way, the highest score counts.

          Tsumo answers read dealer pays / each non-dealer pays. When the dealer wins by tsumo, all three pay the same ("all"). There is one dora indicator and no ura-dora, red fives, honba or riichi sticks. There is no kiriage mangan either, so 4 han 30 fu pays 7700. The rules guide has the full yaku and fu tables.
