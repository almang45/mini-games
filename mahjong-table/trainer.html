How answers are scored

Shanten is how many tiles a hand is from tenpai (one tile from winning). The best cut leaves the lowest shanten; among those, the one with the most ukeire wins — the number of still-unseen tiles that would lower shanten again. Ties all count as correct.

Seven pairs and thirteen orphans count, as on the Riichi table. Only your own 14 tiles count as seen: there are no discards, calls or dora here, and hand value (yaku) isn't weighed.
