Backgammon

Move all fifteen checkers round the board into your home board (bottom right), then bear them off; the first to bear off all fifteen wins. Each die moves one checker that many points; doubles move four times. You can't land on a point held by two or more enemy checkers. Land on a single one to hit it to the bar — a checker on the bar must come back in before anything else moves. Use both dice if you can. Click a checker, then where it goes. A gammon (the loser bore off none) scores 2, a backgammon (and still has a checker on the bar or in the winner's home board) scores 3.
